Privacy Policy

Happy Day Movers · Last updated November 6, 2026

Happy Day Movers (“we,” “us”) respects your privacy. This policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and your choices.

Information we collect

When you request a quote, book a move or contact us, we collect your name, phone number, email address, moving addresses, move date and details about the items you are moving.

How we use it

We use your information to give you quotes, schedule and complete your move, send updates about your move, and answer your questions.

Text messages (SMS)

If you give us your mobile number and agree to receive texts, we will send messages about your quote, booking, move-day updates and customer care. Message frequency varies. Message and data rates may apply. Reply STOP to opt out at any time, or HELP for help.

No mobile information will be shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. Text messaging opt-in data and consent are not shared with any third parties.

Sharing

We do not sell your personal information. We share it only with service providers who help us run our business (such as our phone, scheduling and payment systems), and only as needed to serve you, or when required by law.

Security

We use reasonable safeguards to protect your information.

Contact us

Questions about this policy? Email info@happydaymovers.com (info@happydaymovers.com).
