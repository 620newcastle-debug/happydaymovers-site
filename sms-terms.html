SMS Terms & Conditions

Happy Day Movers · Last updated November 6, 2026

Program

Happy Day Movers (“we,” “us”) sends text messages to customers who have requested a moving quote or booked a move with us and have agreed to receive texts.

What we send

Quote follow-ups, booking confirmations, move-date reminders, crew arrival updates on moving day, and customer care replies. We do not send marketing messages through this program.

Message frequency

Message frequency varies, typically up to 5 messages per week while your move is being planned and completed.

Costs

Message and data rates may apply. Check with your mobile carrier for details. Carriers are not liable for delayed or undelivered messages.

Opt out

Reply STOP at any time to stop receiving texts. You will receive one confirmation message and no further messages. Reply START to resubscribe.

Help

Reply HELP for help, or call us at (800) 336-2424 ((800) 336-2424).

Privacy

We never sell or share your mobile number or opt-in information with third parties for marketing. See our Privacy Policy.
